Privacy in short

  • No account, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • The site keeps nothing you type or choose in its pages.
  • Some pages make your browser contact other services, listed below with what they receive.

Hosting

Vercel serves the pages. Like any web server, it receives your IP address, the address of the page requested, your browser's description (user agent) and the page you came from, and keeps them in its logs to run and protect the service, under its privacy policy. Vercel is in the United States and relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The fonts and the map library are served by the site itself, not by third parties.

Third-party services contacted by your browser

Each of these services receives, like any website, your IP address and your browser's description, plus what is listed, and processes them under its own privacy policy.

Cinéma

  • Letterboxd (Letterboxd Limited, New Zealand): the film posters are loaded from its image servers, which also see the address of this site as the referring page.

Publications

  • HAL (CCSD, France), api.archives-ouvertes.fr: the list of publications is requested when the page opens.
  • doi.org (DOI Foundation) and Crossref: only when you open a publication's BibTeX, the publication's DOI.

Rendez-vous

  • OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation, United Kingdom), tile.openstreetmap.org: the map images, hence the area you look at.
  • IGN Géoplateforme (Institut national de l'information géographique et forestière, France), data.geopf.fr: what you type in an address field (from 3 characters), and your position when you click « ma position actuelle », to find its address.
  • openrouteservice (HeiGIT gGmbH, Germany), api.openrouteservice.org: the positions of the participants and of the candidate places, to get walking, cycling and driving times and paths, with the API key you entered.
  • Transitous (community project, transitous.org), api.transitous.org: the same positions with the arrival date and time slot, to get public transport journeys (default choice).
  • Tisséo (Toulouse public transport, France), api.tisseo.fr: only if you enter a Tisséo key and choose Tisséo, the same positions and times with your key, instead of Transitous.

The participants' names never leave your browser. The addresses you enter may be other people's: they go to these services as described, so a nearby landmark (a metro station, a square) works as well as an exact home address.

Sport and the other pages

The sport and cinema data (from intervals.icu, Letterboxd and The Movie Database) are fetched by the site's own server, not by your browser: these services receive nothing about you. The home page contacts no third party. Links to other sites (GitHub, LinkedIn, Strava…) send nothing until you follow them.

Cookies and local storage

The site sets no cookies, so there is no consent banner. The Rendez-vous page stores the API keys you enter in your browser's local storage, on your device only: each key is sent to its own service, never to this site. « Effacer les clés » or clearing your browser data removes them. This storage only serves a feature you ask for, which exempts it from consent (article 82 of the French Data Protection Act). Your position is only read when you click « ma position actuelle », after your browser asks your permission.

Your rights

These transfers rest on the legitimate interest of running the site and providing the features you use (article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). The GDPR gives you rights over data about you (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection); here is where such data actually is:

  • With me: nothing, unless you write to me. The site has no database, no accounts and no logs of its own. If you send me an email, I keep it as long as our exchange needs it, and delete it if you ask.
  • With the host: Vercel's logs, kept and deleted under its own policy. I cannot read them by visitor; you can write to Vercel at privacy@vercel.com, or ask me to pass your request on.
  • With the third-party services listed above: each answers for what it receives, under its own privacy policy; write to it directly.
